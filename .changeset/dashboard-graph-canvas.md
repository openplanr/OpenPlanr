The dashboard Graph fills the window and pans and zooms smoothly to thousands of items. Drag or scroll to move around; pinch or Ctrl/Cmd+scroll zooms around the pointer, including over cards, and never zooms the page. Below 40% zoom the Graph switches to an overview of the whole project. Lenses, type and done toggles and Fit sit in the tool pill, the focus summary in the navigation panel and the minimap in the bottom corner; clicking the minimap moves the view. F now fits the selection into view without changing the lens; focusing on an item's lineage is the Focus button in the navigation panel. On phones the Graph tools sit under the top bar and the minimap and outline are in More.
