Redesign Operate Today on desktop for projects with completed local reviews. The page opens on the latest review: its date and cycle, how many decisions need you, and how many seats reported. The first decision shows its confidence, evidence gaps, kept dissent, first step, expected result, what happens if you wait, and any dissent, with `/planr:operate` ready to copy. "Waiting on you" lists the review's decisions, actions and open questions, filtered by kind; select a row and press C to copy its ID and title. Executive seats shows each of the seven seats as reported, dissenting or waiting, and Evidence lists what's verified and what isn't proven yet. Today, Inbox, Cycles and History tabs sit in the top bar.
