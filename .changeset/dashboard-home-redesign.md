Give the dashboard Home a new layout. The header says how many items need you and how many specs changed today, with a status legend beside it. Attention cards sit on a 12-column grid, and each row shows one detail: how much waits on it, how long it has been quiet, or its priority and filed date. Changed today groups work by spec, Specs in flight shows a progress bar for each spec, and with no active sprint you get the `/planr:sprint` command to copy. Selecting a row opens a floating bar with its command, and C copies it. The "Since your last visit" line is gone from Home.
