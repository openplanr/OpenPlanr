Give the dashboard Board a new layout. A View switch replaces the Columns and Lanes menus and remembers your choice. Hide done is now a checkbox, and done work folds into a summary card. Each card shows only what it knows: what it waits on, what it unblocks, or when it was last updated. Long columns end with a "Show more" link. Selecting a card opens a floating bar with its command, ready to copy. On a phone you pick one status at a time from a row of tabs.
