Fit Operate Today to tablets and phones. Between 600 and 1199px wide the page is one column, with Executive seats and Evidence side by side under the decision and the list of what's waiting. On a phone the header drops the date and the Run a review button, the decision keeps its brief with compact Confidence, Gaps and Dissent tiles, and Executive seats and Evidence become rows that open in place. When a decision is waiting, the phone hides the "Waiting on you" list and puts `/planr:operate` in a bar above the navigation with a Copy command button; a governed next step stays inside the decision card. Focus rings sit outside Today's links and buttons, and inside its rows and the phone rows that open.
