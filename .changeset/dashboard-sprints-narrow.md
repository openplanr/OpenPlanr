Fit the dashboard Sprints page to tablets and phones. Between 600 and 1199px wide the page keeps the desktop layout, with item columns that line up from row to row. On a phone the active sprint shows its ID, the current day and its title, then its done count, capacity and status bar. It lists four open items, blocked and carried ones first, and "All N items" opens the rest in place. Closed sprints sit behind a "Past sprints" row, and the timeline is hidden. Below 900px, tapping an item selects it and shows its command; Open in the selection bar opens the Inspector, and Escape returns you to the item.
