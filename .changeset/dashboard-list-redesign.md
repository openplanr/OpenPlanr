Redesign the dashboard List on desktop. The title shows how many items match, with the presets beside it. Search, the Type, Status, Priority, Label and Group filters, density and the filter link now sit on one row, and every active filter shows as a chip you can clear, including filters that came from a link. The "List options" panel is gone. Select rows with their checkboxes, or all filtered rows from the header, and a floating bar shows each item's command, ready to copy with C. The bulk target-status picker and the sprint command button are gone, and so is the keyboard hints row. Shortcuts stay under `?`.
