Fit the dashboard Activity page to tablets and phones. Between 600 and 1199px wide the changes come first, with "Since you opened" and "Busiest specs" below them: side by side from 900px, stacked under it, where the chart is hidden. On a phone the kinds are a row of pills you can scroll, and "More filters" opens a sheet with Type, range and Spec, showing how many are set. Each change is two lines: the ID, what changed and the time, then the title. The chart and the side cards are hidden. Below 900px, tapping a change selects it and shows its command; Open in the selection bar opens the Inspector. A quiet page at 90 days no longer offers to show 90 days, a view with several types shows "Type: N types", and an item removed while the page is open shows as removed in every row right away.
