Two new skills, both run only when you invoke them. `/planr:share` turns the status report, the current sprint or the latest release notes into a team update, shows you the exact text and destination, and creates a chat draft for you to send once you approve; with no chat connected it prints the update ready to paste. `/planr:connect` reports which chat, project tracker and source control connections your host has and shows how to connect the missing ones through the host. Neither asks for or stores a credential.
