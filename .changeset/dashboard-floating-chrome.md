From 600px wide, the dashboard's navigation, tools, status and inspector float over the page instead of taking fixed columns. The menu pill holds every view, search, keyboard shortcuts and the theme, and opens an optional navigation panel you can pin. The tool pill carries the view's controls with their shortcuts; on the List, 1 to 3 group by spec, status or type, F focuses the filter and D switches density. The action pill shows Read-only or Governed actions, the Live state and the sidebar toggle, which marks a selection. The sidebar has Inspector and Outline tabs and opens with Enter or the toggle. The round help button shows the build, branch, totals and shortcuts in place of the status bar. Alt+[ and Alt+] open the navigation panel and the sidebar, Alt+Z hides all chrome, and F6 moves between regions. Pinned panels are remembered per project. Phones keep the top bar, bottom navigation and full-height inspector.
