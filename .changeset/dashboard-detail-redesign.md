Redesign the dashboard Detail page on desktop. It keeps the navigation rail and top bar of the other Plan views, and the top bar shows where you opened it from, its spec and its ID. The header shows the ID, a status such as "Blocked by T-109", the priority and the updated date, with Copy link beside the title. The trace runs from the spec and story through this item to what it unblocks, and a spec shows how many stories and tasks it holds. Acceptance criteria, Files and the Description sit on the left; Next step and Properties sit on the right. Next step names what to finish first and gives its command to copy. Properties list only values that exist, a missing reference shows as a warning, and the frontmatter stays collapsed. Placeholders such as "Unknown", "none" and "Not recorded" are gone, and so are the "More relationships" lists, the spec report command and the read status line.
