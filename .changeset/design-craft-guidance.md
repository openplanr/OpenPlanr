The design skills now push for a deliberate look instead of generic defaults. A design names its direction and the one thing a viewer should remember. New products without a brand start from two to four quick direction sketches for you to pick from. Type and color stay within a small system, common generic choices are named and avoided, and landing pages follow a clear order with marked placeholders for facts only you can supply.

Designs can also use real typefaces. When a new font is needed, the skill saves openly licensed font files into the design, up to two families with three weights each, so the design still opens offline and fetches nothing. When it can't save them, it uses a named system font stack and tells you.
