The design skills now push for a deliberate look instead of generic defaults. A design names its direction and the one thing a viewer should remember. New products without a brand start from two to four quick direction sketches for you to pick from, unless you leave the choice to the skill. Type and color stay within a small system, common generic choices are named and avoided, and landing pages follow a clear order, with realistic sample prices and quotes marked as samples for you to replace.

Designs can also carry real typefaces. Openly licensed font files saved in a design are embedded when it renders, so it still opens offline and fetches nothing; without them, the design uses a named system font stack and says which one.
