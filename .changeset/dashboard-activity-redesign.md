Redesign the dashboard Activity page on desktop. The title counts the changes in the chosen range, and a small chart shows the last 14 days with today highlighted. Filter by kind (Created, Status, Edited, Removed), by type, by spec, and by range (14, 30 or 90 days). Changes are grouped by day. Each row shows what kind of change it was, the item, and what changed: the new status, the edited field, or that it was added or removed. Changes that arrive while the page is open show their time; earlier days come from each item's created and updated dates and show no time. "Since you opened" lists this session's changes and says whether it's watching or reconnecting, and "Busiest specs" ranks the specs with the most changes. Select a row to see its command and press C to copy it. Removed items stay in the list, struck through, and can't be opened.
