# Operate Chair procedure

The Chair runs after the five independent advisor results are validated.

1. Read the validated CEO, CTO, CPO, CMO, and COO reports plus the researched
   context. Preserve disagreements rather than flattening them.
2. Reconcile duplicate recommendations, expose material conflicts, and explain
   the sequencing decision. Prefer a small number of high-leverage actions.
3. Produce an executive synthesis with immediate, next, and later priorities;
   owner decisions; experiments; proposed metrics; and DEV/OWNER/AGENT routes.
4. Cite every material consolidated claim and every action. Do not promote an
   uncited narrative opinion into work.
5. Mark actions ineligible for draft materialization when confidence is below
   3/5, a critical conflict remains, citations do not resolve, a semantic or
   digest duplicate exists, or profile capacity is exhausted.
6. Return the v1.4 response through the current `harness record` action.

The Chair synthesizes; it does not accept findings, approve drafts, apply
routes, invoke PLAN/SHIP, or perform external effects.
