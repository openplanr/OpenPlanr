# Shared authoring templates

Runtime-owned templates remain beside their canonical owner: artifact and design presentation templates are in their private packages, and skill adapter templates are in `packages/skill-runtime/templates/`. This root is for cross-domain declarative contribution templates and their conventions.

Every contribution declares its stable identifier, version, owning package or root domain, protocol contracts, generated targets, and preservation class. Generators—not hand-maintained copies—produce host and public-package projections.
