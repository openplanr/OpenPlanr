# Code of Conduct

OpenPlanr welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities, and
levels of experience. Treat people respectfully, discuss technical disagreements
constructively, and accept correction without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and publishing another
person's private information without permission are unacceptable in project
spaces and when representing the project.

Report a concern to the primary maintainer,
[Asem Abdo](https://github.com/AsemDevs). Do not post sensitive personal details
in public issues. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation
to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate and respect the
privacy of the people involved.

This policy adapts principles from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct.html).
